# STEP export status — TILE T1

T1 native source is tile-t1.scad; 3MF and STL compatibility exports are generated by scripts/export-tile-t1.py. The installed OpenSCAD 2021.01 exporter supports 3MF/STL but not B-rep STEP. No FreeCAD, CadQuery, OpenCascade, Fusion or other STEP-capable CAD runtime is installed in this workspace.

No placeholder STEP was generated. A mesh relabeled as STEP would not be an editable solid and would be misleading for manufacturing. Export real AP214/AP242 STEP from the same controlled T1 geometry only after opening the native model in a B-rep-capable CAD system or rebuilding it in a selected native B-rep CAD package. Re-run component-envelope and assembly checks after conversion and record the translator/version and source hash.

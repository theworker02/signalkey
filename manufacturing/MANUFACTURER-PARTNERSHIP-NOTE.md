# Manufacturer partnership note — SignalKey TILE T1

SignalKey supplies this repository's TILE T1 concept, prototype-oriented CAD, drawings, component-envelope information and test records as a starting point for a potential manufacturing partnership. It is **not** a released manufacturing data package, purchase authorization, production specification or instruction to begin tooling or build units.

The manufacturer or manufacturing-development partner is expected to perform the majority of the work needed to turn this proposal into a buildable product. That work includes, at minimum:

- Validate the received electronics, harness, connector, switch, LED and optical components against the enclosure; establish controlled component drawings and tolerances.
- Complete DFM/DFA review; rebuild or convert the approved concept into controlled production B-rep CAD; establish draft, shrink, wall/rib/boss design, parting line, gates, ejectors, undercuts and tool actions.
- Select and qualify production materials, colour, texture, optical material, fasteners, inserts, feet, cable retention and assembly fixtures.
- Produce toleranced part and assembly drawings, a released BOM with approved suppliers/alternates, work instructions, inspection plans, process controls and traceability requirements.
- Design and validate tooling, fixtures, electrical/functional test equipment, ESD controls, programming/provisioning steps and any robotic operations.
- Build and evaluate prototypes, engineering samples and first articles; complete mechanical, electrical, optical, reliability, safety and regulatory testing appropriate to the intended markets.
- Identify manufacturing risks, costs, lead times, yield assumptions and required design changes, then obtain written SignalKey approval before changing the product definition or beginning production.

SignalKey will provide design intent, software/firmware context, prototype evidence, review decisions and collaboration during this work. Physical test evidence, supplier dimensions and production qualifications not present in this repository remain open work; they must not be inferred from the concept render, STL/3MF files, digital mesh checks or documentation.

## Partnership use

Prospective partners may use these materials to assess capability, prepare a development scope, identify missing inputs and propose a validation/tooling plan. Do not quote, order components, cut tools, fabricate production quantities, represent compliance, or begin commercial production without a separate written agreement and a jointly approved production release.

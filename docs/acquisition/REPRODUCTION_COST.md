## Why acquisition beats greenfield

Reproducing `signalkey` means rediscovering domain constraints, re-implementing architecture and edge cases, rebuilding tests/CI/packaging, and rewriting diligence materials already present in this repository. Buying the transferable IP is the faster path to a production-ready starting point.

## Product thesis

A local-first desktop workflow controller with a tactile hardware direction. SignalKey lets a person press a physical control, run an explicitly approved local workflow, and inspect its observed result. The desktop companion is at 0.1.0-alpha.2: the physical product is an engineering concept under development.
